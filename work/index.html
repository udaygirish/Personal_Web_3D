He, Crasta, Maradana, Varadarajan, Tanaka, Liu — ORS 2025

Introduces an AI-based Ultrasound Tracking System (AUTS) using SAM for segmentation and PIPs for point tracking to measure Medial Patellofemoral Distance frame-by-frame from ultrasound videos. Tested on 42 knees with strong agreement against manual measurements.

MinNav: Minimalist Navigation using Optical Flow for Active Tiny Aerial Robots

Patil, Singh, Maradana, Sanket — ICRA 2026

MinNav is a navigation stack based on optical flow and its uncertainty enabling tiny aerial robots to fly through scenes with static and dynamic obstacles and unknown-shaped gaps, without any prior knowledge of scene components. Demonstrates 70% success rate across diverse real-world environments.

Optical Flow UAV Navigation Robotics Computer Vision
