Research Agenda

🛸

Active Perception

Developing minimal-computation navigation pipelines for tiny robots. By leveraging monocular optical flow uncertainty and active robot exploration, systems can navigate cluttered, dynamic environments without high-power metric depth sensors.

🦾

Robot Manipulation

Applying end-to-end reinforcement learning and language-grounded vision models to multi-DOF robotic arms. The goal is to enable complex pick-and-place tasks under highly variable real-world conditions.

🩺

Clinical CV

Pioneering deep learning solutions in medical imaging. Integrating Segment Anything Models (SAM) and point tracking algorithms to perform dynamic frame-by-frame ultrasound analysis for biomechanical assessment.

Scientific Work

In Review

An Artificial Intelligence Approach for Automated Dynamic Ultrasound Measurement in the Assessment of Patellar Instability

He, Xingxin; Crasta, Nikitha; Maradana, Uday Girish; Varadarajan, Kartik Mangudi; Tanaka, Miho J.; Liu, Fang

Introduces AUTS (AI-based Ultrasound Tracking System) for dynamic assessment of patellofemoral joint function. Using SAM and point tracking, AUTS measures Medial Patellofemoral Distance frame-by-frame, providing high diagnostic reliability for patellar instability.

Accepted ICRA 2026 / Submitted IEEE RAL

MinNav: Minimalist Navigation using Optical flow for Active Tiny Aerial Robots

Aniket Patil, Mandeep Singh, Uday Girish Maradana, Nitin J. Sanket

Proposes a navigation stack based on monocular optical flow uncertainty to guide active nano-drones through static and dynamic obstacles and gaps without metric depth sensors, executing all calculations onboard on limited hardware.
